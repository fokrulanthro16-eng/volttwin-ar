# Security & Safety Compliance Policy

## Industrial High-Voltage Safety Disclaimer

> **DANGER: HIGH VOLTAGE ELECTRICITY (480V+ 3-PHASE) CAN CAUSE FATAL ELECTRIC SHOCK AND CATASTROPHIC ARC-FLASH EXPLOSIONS.**

VoltTwin AR v2.0 is a **spatial guidance and augmented reality verification copilot** intended to assist qualified electrical personnel who are trained in NFPA 70E, OSHA 1910.147, and site-specific Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) protocols.

1. **Physical PPE Mandate:** The use of VoltTwin AR v2.0 does NOT substitute for mandatory personal protective equipment (PPE), including arc-rated flash suits, safety face shields, 1000V-rated insulated gloves with leather protectors, and calibrated multimeters.
2. **Physical Contact Verification:** Even with augmented reality ghost probes and automated discharge countdowns, physical contact with electrical conductors must ALWAYS be verified with a physically calibrated, category-rated test instrument following standard "test-before-touch" protocols.

We take the safety and cybersecurity of industrial control systems and AR safety tooling with the utmost seriousness.

If you discover a software vulnerability, cryptographic flaw, or safety-critical edge case in VoltTwin AR v2.0:

1. **Do Not File a Public GitHub Issue.**
2. Send an encrypted advisory to the core safety team at:  
   **`safety@volttwin.ar`** or **`fokrulanthro16@gmail.com`**
3. Include the following details in your advisory:
   * Affected component (e.g., Finite State Machine, SIK Raycasting, Cryptographic Token Generator).
   * Reproduction steps or proof of concept.

## Cryptographic Token Integrity & Audit Compliance

All digital work-order permits and audit hashes generated by VoltTwin AR (such as `LOTO-HASH-994A`) utilize deterministic SHA-256 HMAC digest validation. 

Tampering with audit JSON payloads or attempting to forge zero-energy certificates violates industrial compliance regulations (OSHA CFR 1910.147) and will cause cryptographic signature verification to fail immediately.
