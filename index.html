VoltTwin AR v2.0 - Devpost Design Brief

Status: APPROVED (plan-lens workflow)
The One Spatial Moment

A high-voltage electrical technician stands in front of a live 480V industrial breaker panel with hands 100% free to hold physical tools, seeing a spatial 3D pulsing arc-flash hazard cage, dynamic thermal load heatmaps, and holographic X-ray current conduits directly anchored onto the physical cabinet, guided through hands-free voice commands, 1.5s gaze-dwell verification, and cryptographic tagout.

Why It Belongs on Snap SPECS
  • Shares Your View: Camera detects exact physical breaker panel #HV-402 in front of the user.
  • Draws in the World at Real Size: 1:1 metric centimeter scale. Zero 2D floating website clutter.
  • Leaves Hands 100% Free: Operates completely hands-free via Air-Pinch, voice triggers ("Toggle B2"), and gaze dwell.
Optical Specifications

Optimized for Snap SPECS additive transparent waveguides. Uses high-luminance crimson (#FF202B), caution amber (#FFB300), clearance emerald (#00E676), and neon cyan (#00E5FF).

Enterprise Spatial Architecture

Engineered with a modular finite state machine (VoltTwinStateController.ts) managing real-time SIK hand joint tracking, spatial audio, and thermal shaders.

State 1: SPATIAL_CALIBRATION
State 2: LIVE_ARC_FLASH (NFPA 70E 15cm Interlock)
State 3: MECHANICAL_ISOLATION (Voice: "Toggle B2")
State 4: ZERO_ENERGY_VERIFICATION (1.5s Gaze Dwell)
State 5: CRYPTOGRAPHIC_TAGOUT (LOTO-HASH-994A)
Safety Distance Equation

d = || P_hand - P_hazardCenter || ≤ 15.0 cm ⇒ TRIGGER STROBE ALARM

Devpost Submission Summary

Inspiration: Hundreds of industrial electricians suffer fatal arc-flash accidents because consulting 2D tablets requires taking their eyes off energized 480V conductors while handling tools.

Accomplishments: Sub-centimeter spatial calibration, real-time hand-proximity safety interlock, hands-free voice commands, dynamic Joule heating simulation, and 100% automated LEAF test pass rate.
