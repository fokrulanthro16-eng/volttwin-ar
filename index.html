VoltTwin AR v2.0 - Official Design Brief

Approval Status: APPROVED (plan-lens workflow)
The One Spatial Moment

A high-voltage electrical technician stands in front of a live 480V industrial breaker panel with hands 100% free to hold physical multimeters, seeing a spatial 3D pulsing arc-flash hazard cage, dynamic thermal heatmaps, and holographic X-ray conduits directly anchored onto the physical cabinet, guided through hands-free voice commands, 1.5s gaze-dwell verification, and cryptographic tagout.

3 Core Pillars of AR Glasses
  • Shares Your View: Camera detects physical breaker panel #HV-402 in front of the user and tracks gaze dwell on Terminal T1.
  • Draws in the World at Real Size: 1:1 metric centimeter scale. Zero 2D floating website clutter.
  • Leaves Hands 100% Free: Operates completely hands-free via Air-Pinch, voice triggers ("Toggle B2", "Explode View"), and gaze dwell.
Additive Optical Specifications

Optimized for Snap SPECS additive transparent waveguides. Uses high-luminance crimson (#FF202B), caution amber (#FFB300), clearance emerald (#00E676), and neon cyan (#00E5FF).
